# German-to-English PDF review exercise

Use this short, fictional [German source PDF](german-english-review-exercise.pdf) to practice checking a translation against its original. The sample is a teaching exercise written for this project, not a real study or a Scanfile translation result. Sample PDF and text on this page: CC BY 4.0, Scanfile.

> Die Ergebnisse zeigen keinen signifikanten Unterschied; die Stichprobe umfasste 1.500 Teilnehmende.

One possible English rendering is: “The results show no significant difference; the sample included 1,500 participants.” This rendering is hand-written, not generated by the product. A version saying there **was** a significant difference would reverse the negation. Depending on context, German `1.500` may mean English `1,500`; German `1,5` may correspond to English `1.5`. Check the actual document's conventions and units rather than automatically changing punctuation.

The 2021 paper [As Easy as 1, 2, 3: Behavioural Testing of NMT Systems for Numerical Translation](https://aclanthology.org/2021.findings-acl.415/) investigates number-translation errors, including decimal and thousands separators. It studied the systems in that paper, not Scanfile; it is a reason to check numbers, not evidence of this product's error rate.

To try the workflow, download the source PDF and translate it with a service you choose. Check its privacy terms before uploading. Compare its result to the original and note whether the negation, participant count and sentence structure survived. This one simple, selectable-text page cannot establish how a system handles scans, tables or other German documents, and neither this exercise nor a fluent result certifies accuracy.

The [local review reader](https://scanfile.online/tools/pdf-translator#compare-pdf-translation) can open the original and your translated PDF side by side without uploading the files used for comparison; site analytics are separate. The Python tool in this repository can also make a local two-column review PDF, within the limits described in the [README](../README.md#make-a-review-copy). Both require you to select corresponding pages and judge the meaning yourself.
