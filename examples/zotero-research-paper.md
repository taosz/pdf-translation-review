# Review a translated research PDF alongside its Zotero attachment

This manual workflow keeps the original and a translated copy under the same Zotero item. It is useful when you need to read a whole paper and already have a PDF you are permitted to process. It does not require a Zotero plugin. Menu names may vary by version and platform.

## Choose a workflow

- **A few passages while reading:** a selection-translation plugin such as [Translate for Zotero](https://github.com/windingwind/zotero-pdf-translate) may be more convenient. Check its provider, privacy and costs before use.
- **A whole file in Zotero:** an integrated full-document plugin may avoid manually moving files, but inspect its service, privacy, pricing, language and output limitations. For example, [Geyi's plugin](https://www.geyi.net/en/zotero-plugin) attaches a result to the item and states its own plan requirements.
- **A separate translation to review:** use the steps below. Scanfile is one possible service; its developer maintains this repository. Scanfile is a website, not a Zotero plugin, and does not attach files to your library automatically.

## Get the original and translate it

1. In Zotero, expand the paper's bibliographic item and find its PDF attachment. If it is not downloaded on this device, make it available locally first. In desktop Zotero, right-click the attachment and choose **Show File** to locate it; Zotero also documents dragging stored attachments out of the library. See [Zotero's attachment documentation](https://www.zotero.org/support/attaching_files).
2. Check that you have permission to process the file and that sending it to a third party is consistent with your institution's rules. Avoid uploading confidential or unpublished material without authorization. Choose a service suitable for the document's sensitivity and review its current terms.
3. For the Scanfile option, open the [PDF translator](https://scanfile.online/tools/pdf-translator#research-paper-translation), choose the PDF, source and target languages, and inspect the selected pages and displayed credit estimate before confirming. It sends the selected file to the translation service; it is not an offline translator. An eligible free trial covers the first three **file** pages, while other pages require the credits shown in the product. File page positions can differ from printed journal page numbers.
4. Download the finished translation. A sample or selected-pages output may contain fewer pages than the original. Do not replace the original; keep a clearly named separate copy so that the source remains available for verification.

## Attach and compare

5. Drag the downloaded PDF onto the original bibliographic item in Zotero, or use the item's attachment controls to add the file. Confirm that it appears as a **child attachment of that item**, rather than as a separate bibliographic record. Zotero may rename stored attachments according to its settings; check the file before deleting any local copy. Stored attachments may sync if file syncing is enabled, so apply the same privacy rules to the translated copy. See [Zotero's file storage documentation](https://www.zotero.org/support/attaching_files).
6. Compare corresponding pages manually. The [local browser review reader](https://scanfile.online/tools/pdf-translator#compare-pdf-translation) lets you open the original and the translated PDF and choose their pages separately; the two selected review files stay in the browser, while site analytics are separate. If the output covers only original pages 8–10, for example, its first page may correspond to original page 8. Page numbers are file positions, not necessarily printed numbers.
7. Check methods, uncertainty and negation against the original. Inspect table headings, units, figures, captions, references and equations; translation or layout can alter them. Note unresolved passages. Cite the original paper and follow the relevant citation rules; a machine translation is not independent evidence or a certified translation.

## Try with a public example

The [fictional English scanned brief](https://scanfile.online/samples/pdf/scanned-en-pt/reading-brief.en.pdf) and its [actual Portuguese output](https://scanfile.online/samples/pdf/scanned-en-pt/reading-brief.pt.pdf) are a one-page example for practicing comparison. They are not a real paper or a benchmark of translation quality. You can practice the comparison without adding them to Zotero. The browser review reader compares only the files you choose; it does not determine semantic equivalence, perform OCR, or certify accuracy.

For an offline two-column PDF, see this repository's [review command](../README.md#make-a-review-copy). Its inputs must be unencrypted PDFs within the listed size and page limits. Creating a visual review copy can alter interactive and accessibility features, so retain the source files.
